## 1 and 2. Patterns, and what the tables show

**Working backward from the conclusion.**
- Your sentence: "I want to drop oil and gas and fund the contracted wind and solar requests only." The preference comes first and the reasons follow.
- A5 confirms it. You left off the table the mixed book and a smaller renewables position inside book D.
- The mixed book D has 26 members, $5.20B capex, expected NPV $15.9B, and a CVaR95 loss of -4.98 (the worst 5% is still a profit). Its chance of a loss is 0.00.
- Leases only has 20 members, $1.37B capex, expected NPV $15.6B, CVaR95 loss -4.92, and a loss chance of 0.00.
- Power only has 3 members, $2.12B capex, expected NPV $0.10B, CVaR95 loss 0.02 (a loss), and a loss chance of 0.71.
- Your book was ranked last on expected NPV and the only one with a positive tail loss, before the alternatives were considered.

**Moving the goalposts.**
- Your A2: "the committee wants a book that holds its value." Your statement: "a book of renewables is what our lenders want to see." Your A3: "I want the company to be seen as part of the transition."
- The criterion moved from value to appearance.
- On value, power only returns $0.10B against $15.9B for book D and $15.6B for leases only.
- On safety, power only loses money in 0.71 of the held-out scenarios.

**Double standard and cherry-picking.**
- You judge wind on the contracted case. The contracted NPVs are $24.9M, $25.7M, $20.0M, $27.6M and $21.9M, and you cite them.
- You pass over the merchant case for the same five wind requests: -$38.0M, -$38.6M, -$32.1M, -$43.0M and -$34.1M. The contract alone decides the sign.
- You say "wind and solar", but your A4 admits you passed over solar. Under contract the five solar requests are -$8.01M, -$17.2M, -$8.75M, -$10.6M and -$8.49M.
- The leases get the opposite treatment. Your "stranded within ten years" has no table behind it. The tables hold no ten-year transition scenario, and I cannot give one.
- What the stress table shows for the leases: $14.6B in base, $14.6B under the oil collapse (2020 path), and $12.9B in the simultaneous downside. The book total in that downside is $12.1B, a change of -1.76.
- The default power book is negative in every stress. It is -$0.76B in base and -$0.86B in the simultaneous downside.

**Treating a FORECAST as if it were observed.**
- Your sentence: "the PPA makes wind safe." A PPA fixes the price. It does not build the project.
- The chance of operating within seven years is a FORECAST. For the wind requests it is 0.13, 0.15, 0.09, 0.11 and 0.11. For the solar requests it is 0.06 to 0.08.
- Your A1 calls "none is built" the worst case. By the table, most requests not operating within seven years is the central outcome.
- The contracted PPA terms are not shown in the tables, so we cannot check the price behind the contracted NPVs.

**Defending a past position.**
- Your A3: "I have argued that position in public; that is a past position I am defending."
- You asked about deposits, and A1 names "deposits and study costs", but no table holds a deposit figure. We cannot size your worst case in dollars. Your A1 gave no dollar figure either.
- The tables do give the tail of the book nearest yours: a $0.02B loss at the 5% tail. That is small, and it supports the "thin tail" point. The weakness is the 0.71 chance of a loss and the $0.10B expected NPV, not the tail.

**Assumed stakeholders.**
- Your A2 names no party you spoke to. The lender's wish for "the PPA and a thin tail" and the committee's wish are assumptions.
- The cap also works against the book. With a 40% cap per technology, two technologies cannot absorb the whole $6.15B budget. Solar is negative under contract, so filling the budget means adding solar or leaving money unspent.

**Provenance.**
- Your case rests on FORECAST timing (the chance of operating), on contracted NPVs whose PPA terms the tables do not show, and on a stranding claim with no table.
- The leases you reject rest on ASSUMED costs, with FORECAST production and DERIVED NPV. Their data completeness is 0.80, against 1.00 for the requests. That is a real weakness.
