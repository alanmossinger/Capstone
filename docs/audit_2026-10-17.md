# Independent technical audit — report v6 and the code behind it (2026-10-17)

Scope: `report/report.md` v6, `src/`, notebooks 01–10, `results/`, `app/`, `tests/` at commit `61a8177`.
Every number below was printed by a script run against the committed outputs; where a number came from a
re-solve or a refit, the method is named. Nothing in `results/` was changed by the audit.

Severity: **CRITICAL** changes a headline result or the memo's recommendation; **HIGH** a wrong number or a
claim the evidence does not support; **MEDIUM** a methodological weakness to fix or disclose; **LOW** cosmetic.
Type: **BUG** (the code does not do what the registration or the report says) or **METHOD** (it does what it
says, and what it says is weak).

## Summary

The prediction layer holds up: no post-entry feature enters the entry models, the train/test split is clean,
recalibration never sees the scored cohort, and the registered failures are reported as registered. The
problems are downstream, in valuation, allocation and the gym, and three of them change the memo:

1. **The four books were not compared under the same constraints** (A1). Books A and B ignore the 40%
   technology cap that the registration applies to every book. Under the committee grid the whole
   "$1.9B of expected value for a $1.0B smaller tail" is the cap; risk aversion (λ) accounts for $6M of it.
   Held to the same cap, the risk-aware book *beats* top-N NPV by $497M [$427M, $557M] on the registered metric.
2. **Half the power candidates no longer exist** (E1). Of the 20 ERCOT requests, 8 were withdrawn and 2
   were operating by the end of 2024; all five gas requests are resolved, four of them withdrawn in 2019.
   Every book holds gas requests, and the memo tells the committee to fund four of them.
3. **Half the lease baselines never decline** (E2). The Arps fit returns a zero decline for 10 of the 20 leases,
   which then produce at their 2024 rate for twenty years. With an 8%/yr terminal decline the registered lease
   book falls from $14.6B to $8.1B and the committee book from $7.0B to $2.8B.
4. **The gym's edge is a lease-room artefact with hindsight** (G1). $3,724M of the rule's $3,772M edge over
   instinct comes from three lease rooms that use leases chosen on 2024 output, decline fits through 2024 and
   scenarios that revert to the mean of the realized future. The three rooms are one decision repeated.
5. **The AI advisor adds no decision value over the rule** (G5): −$8.0M a situation [−$17.5M, −$1.3M],
   worse in 12 of 16 situations. Its only gain over instinct is matching the optimizer in the lease rooms.

What the corrected system will show cannot be predicted from the audit; the corrections are listed in
`docs/implementation_plan_v8.md` and are run as labelled post-registration corrections beside the
registered record, which stays as it was scored.
